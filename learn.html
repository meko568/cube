Cube Anatomy

Understand the pieces before you turn.

Centers (6)

Fixed pieces that define each face color. They never move relative to each other — white is always opposite yellow, red opposite orange, blue opposite green.

U/D/R/L/F/B

Edges (12)

Two-colored pieces between centers. 12 total. Each belongs to exactly two faces (e.g., white-red edge). Orientation matters — flipped edges are unsolved.

UF, UR, UB, UL, DF, DR, DB, DL, FR, FL, BR, BL

Corners (8)

Three-colored pieces at cube corners. 8 total. Each belongs to three faces (e.g., white-red-blue). Twisted corners are unsolved — orientation is key.

URF, UFL, ULB, UBR, DFR, DLF, DBL, DRB

Standard Notation

Singmaster notation — the universal language of cubing.

Face Turns

U
Up face (white) clockwise 90°
U'
Up face counter-clockwise 90°
U2
Up face 180° (direction irrelevant)
D
Down face (yellow) clockwise 90°
R
Right face (red) clockwise 90°
L
Left face (orange) clockwise 90°
F
Front face (green) clockwise 90°
B
Back face (blue) clockwise 90°

Wide & Slice Moves

r
Wide R — Right two layers together (lowercase on 3x3)
l
Wide L — Left two layers together (lowercase on 3x3)
u
Wide U — Up two layers together (lowercase on 3x3)
M
Middle slice (between L/R) — follows L
E
Equatorial slice (between U/D) — follows D
S
Standing slice (between F/B) — follows F

Cube Rotations

x
Rotate whole cube on R axis (like R)
y
Rotate whole cube on U axis (like U)
z
Rotate whole cube on F axis (like F)

Notation Key

R Clockwise 90°
R' Counter-clockwise 90°
R2 180° turn
r Wide move — 2 layers (lowercase on 3x3; "Rw" is 4x4 notation)
M Slice move (inner layer)
x Whole cube rotation

Practice Drills

Build muscle memory with guided exercises.

Face Recognition

Identify each face by center color. Practice U, R, F, L, B, D turns until automatic.

U R U' R'

Sexy Move

The fundamental 4-move trigger. Returns cube to original state after 6 repetitions.

R U R' U'

Sledgehammer

Mirror of sexy move. Essential for F2L and many OLL/PLL algorithms.

R' F R F'

Four-Spot Pattern

Classic beginner pattern. Visualizes how centers stay fixed while edges/corners move.

M2 E2 S2
