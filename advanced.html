Cross

The foundation of every solve. Plan in inspection, execute blind.

Color-Neutral Cross

Start on any color. Best cross = fewest moves (avg 6–8). Look for easy edges during 15-second inspection.

  • Opposite colors: White/Yellow, Red/Orange, Blue/Green
  • Key technique: Keyhole / multi-slot for extra efficiency
  • Practice: Solve cross blindfolded (just close eyes after inspection)
Example: White Cross (scramble)
F2 U L R' D2
